## 0.9.2 — 2026-10-22

- Rename the app and project to BARO / 晴雨, with the tagline “Half data, half sky.”
- Keep the authored brand copy in GitHub/README; configure Releases/Issue links and seven primary settings with secondary tools under the footer menu.
- Initialize the public source repository with a Gradle wrapper, CI, issue and pull-request templates, contribution and distribution documentation.
- Remove simultaneous bilingual mode; Chinese and English are separate choices, and old bilingual preferences fall back to Chinese.
- Fix the oversized degree symbol and independently positioned C/F by typesetting °C/°F as one unit, adding a clear gap and aligning it to the temperature baseline.
- Unify the complete weather icon family with the controls: 32-unit geometry, bolder shared strokes and fewer decorative details.
- Centralize typography and tighten label/body tracking; retain readable tabular solar times and baseline-aligned temperature units.
- Align the hero to equal columns and shared text baselines, standardize its vertical spacing, size the unframed gear to the title and center cloud-cover readings.
- Enlarge the header date to match the original clock size; increase the gear a further 10% while preserving their shared right edge.
- Require informative English commit titles and detailed descriptions, followed by a short Chinese summary.
- Unify city, air-quality, date and clock typography on one header baseline; increase the title row's top inset.
- Refine fine artwork with an adaptive native-density grid up to 1536 columns, clean geometric contours, softer edge coverage and lower-contrast dithering. Keep the 96-column block style.
- Replace muted accent colours with clear orange, yellow and azure across header dots and meters. Balance natural-blue sky fields and deeper shadows with controlled highlights; keep data surfaces neutral and link cloud-meter gradients to each weather scene; remove fluorescent cyan from broad areas and precipitation.
- Replace the GPU's short master loop with a continuous clock: clouds drift one way over minutes, effects run at independent rates, and successive lightning/meteor events vary in timing and position. Preserve the original natural cloud silhouettes with clean gradients and restrained volume lighting.
- Rebuild the dawn/sunset sea with a level horizon, perspective wavelets, widening broken gold reflections and staggered glints; soften sunlight into broad atmospheric bands.
- Differentiate dawn and sunset composition, cloud proportions, bird passages and reflected light. Add solar granulation, lunar surface detail, brighter three-layer star fields and finer, visibly advecting fog around a broader, volume-shaded island lighthouse with a slowly sweeping warm beacon. Restore rounded dawn cloud proportions and distinguish the windy scene with a smaller, haze-filtered pale-gold sun.
- Attach precipitation to moving cloud bases and mask it from cloud faces. Couple scene transmission, cloud lighting and sun shafts; refine wind into three depths of soft trails while retaining the established flattened cloud style.
- Keep cloud-meter palettes at full track scale, soften short-fill ends and strengthen the percentage symbol. Integrate the fog lighthouse with contact shadows, wet rocks, a feathered shoreline and perspective water ripples. Restrict drifted rain to the current cloud footprint and use all cloud lobes for its base. Give sunrise and sunset distinct diagrams in the same stroke system; the setting fan sinks below a horizon without a directional arrow.
- Retain the existing installation identity and local preferences.
- Pause APK work while the browser design is refined; publish source and repository foundations only. Real weather fetching and verified in-app updating remain pending.
